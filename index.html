Privacy Policy for SoldNearbyUK

Last updated: 26 July 2026

I built SoldNearbyUK as a solo developer, and I'd rather you actually understand what it does with your data than skim a wall of legalese, so here's the plain version.

Your location

The app asks for location permission so it can centre the map on where you are when you open it, or when you tap the locate-me button. That's it — your location gets read once from your phone's location services and used right there on your device to move the map. It never gets sent anywhere, never touches a server of mine (I don't run one), and I never see it. Say no to the permission and the app just opens centred on London instead.

Searching for a postcode or town

Type something into the search bar and it gets sent off to two free, public services to figure out where that is:

  • postcodes.io, for UK postcodes
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim, for place names

That's the one bit of data that does leave your device. It goes straight from your phone to them — I don't sit in the middle of it, log it, or get a copy. Their own privacy policies cover what they do with it on their end.

The map itself

Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, a free public tile service, loaded as you pan and zoom around. Like any website or app loading images, your IP address reaches their servers as part of that — nothing extra gets tacked on by me.

The sold-price data

All the house price data you see is baked into the app itself — it's HM Land Registry's Price Paid Data (public, Crown copyright, Open Government Licence v3.0) combined with the ONS Postcode Directory. It's public record of property transactions, not anything about you.

Things this app just doesn't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no passwords to remember.
  • No ads, and no ad SDKs sitting in the background.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking of any kind.
  • I don't sell or share your data with anyone, because there isn't any of it sitting on my end to sell.
  • No payments — the app is just free.

Children

This app isn't aimed at kids and isn't built to collect anything from them.

If this ever changes

If I update this policy, the new version goes up at this same web address with an updated date at the top — no separate notice, just check back here if you're curious.
